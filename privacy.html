Owlin — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Owlin ("the Software", "we", "us") handles information in connection with the use of our modding utilities. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Owlin is a locally installed, offline utility suite. We do not:

  • Collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information (PII).
  • Track your gameplay activity, session data, or usage statistics.
  • Access your game account credentials or platform login information.
  • Use analytics services, advertising networks, or third-party tracking tools.
  • Send any data to external servers.

3. Local Data Processing

All operations performed by Owlin take place entirely on your local device. Any files read, modified, or generated by the Software — including game saves, configuration files, or logs — remain on your machine and are never transmitted externally.

4. Third-Party Games and Platforms

Owlin may interact with third-party game files and installations. We have no control over the data practices of those games, platforms, or online services. Please refer to the respective privacy policies of the games or platforms you use in conjunction with Owlin.

5. Log Files and Diagnostics

Owlin may generate local log files for debugging and diagnostic purposes. These files are stored only on your device and are not automatically shared with the developers. You may be asked to share them voluntarily when seeking technical support.

6. Children's Privacy

Owlin is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from minors. If you believe a child has used the Software in a way that raises privacy concerns, please contact us.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the Software or applicable regulations. Updates will be published alongside new Software releases. Continued use of Owlin after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to the Owlin development team through the official support channels.
